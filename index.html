ACWS African & Caribbean Wedding Show United Kingdom

One day. The entire culture in one room.

ACWS brings the vibrant African and Caribbean wedding industry together for a single unforgettable day in London — couturiers, royal Aso-Oke and Kente weavers, luxury caterers, makeup artists, photographers and entertainment, all under one historic cathedral roof and all taking bookings on the day.

It is a rare chance to experience the artistry in person. Hand-woven royal textiles you can feel, regal coral beads and diamond bridal jewellery you can try on, gourmet smoky Jollof and Caribbean rum cakes you can taste, and proper conversations with the wedding artisans who will be part of your big day. The runway shows what is debut couture; the exhibition floor is where your wedding gets booked.

Programme Guide

Day Schedule & Runway Line-Up

An exhilarating eight hours structured to give you the ideal balance of planning, tasting, fashion inspiration and celebration.

12:00 PM
Doors Open

Grand Registration & Royal Welcome Reception

Registration opens. VIP attendees receive champagne flutes and luxury bridal gift boxes. Vendor exhibition floors open immediately for consultations. Acoustic Kora and African harp welcome music.

1:15 PM
Catwalk Act I

Runway Presentation: Traditional Heritage & Royal Robes

Show-stopping presentation of ancestral African and Caribbean ceremonies. Featuring authentic royal Aso-Oke, Ghanaian Kente masterpieces, Habesha Kemis, and bespoke ceremonial agbadas.

2:30 PM
Masterclass

Panel: Budgeting, Dual Traditions & Flawless Timing

Live interactive Q&A featuring leading UK multicultural wedding planners and couples who successfully blended two distinct cultural celebrations with grace and elegance.

3:45 PM
Culinary Showcase

Food & Wedding Cake Tasting Hour

The food lounges open for curated tastings. Sample smoky Jollof pairings, Caribbean jerk stations, rum cake varieties, and custom wedding cocktails created by master mixologists.

5:00 PM
Catwalk Act II

Runway Grand Finale: Contemporary Luxury & Reception Glamour

The showstopping white wedding and reception look showcase. International designers present haute couture bridal gowns, modern fusion dresses, diamond tiaras, and reception jumpsuits.

6:30 PM
Live Entertainment

Cultural Dance Spectacular & Live Percussion

Live performances from top diaspora artists, high-energy talking drummers, Caribbean dancers, and an energetic DJ set demonstrating the ultimate reception afterparty experience.

7:45 PM
Grand Prize

Bridal Giveaway Draws & Closing Toast

Announcement of the Luxury Honeymoon Giveaway, vendor lucky draws, and concluding remarks. Doors officially conclude at 8:00 PM.

Questions

Good to know

Do I need to book tickets in advance?

Advance tickets are discounted and guarantee priority entry. Tickets at the door are subject to cathedral venue capacity.

Can I bring my partner, parents or bridal party?

Absolutely! We warmly encourage bringing partners, parents, bridesmaids, groomsmen, and family decision-makers to enjoy the tasting lounges and catwalk together.

What is included with the VIP Royal Experience pass?

VIP ticket holders enjoy fast-track priority entry, reserved front-row seating at all fashion catwalk shows, a chilled glass of champagne upon arrival, an exclusive deluxe gift box (valued at £120+), and access to our private consultation lounge.

Are wedding vendors offering bookings on the day?

Yes. Featured vendors provide exclusive show incentives, reduced date-reservation deposits, and complimentary upgrades for wedding dates booked on the day.

How do I apply as an exhibitor or runway designer?

Select "Book a Stand" or "Exhibit With Us" to open our application form. Our curation team reviews submissions within 48 hours to preserve quality and variety across categories.

How do I reach the venue by public transport?

Lancaster Gate (Central line) is a 4-minute walk; Marble Arch is 8 minutes; Paddington Station (Elizabeth, Bakerloo, Circle, District lines & National Rail) is just 10 minutes away.
