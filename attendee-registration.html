ACWS Privacy Notice

Controller Identity: African & Caribbean Wedding Show (ACWS UK). Contact: privacy@africancaribbeanweddings.co.uk.

1. Purposes & Lawful Bases

  • Ticket Administration & Access: Managing registrations, issuing digital entry passes, and event safety (UK GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) & Art. 6(1)(c)).
  • Event Administration & Curation: Planning venue capacity, runway logistics, and customer support (UK GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Electronic Marketing: Optional promotional updates based strictly on affirmative, opt-in consent (UK GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)).
  • Selected Partner Sharing: Optional partner introductions strictly when you have provided explicit affirmative consent (UK GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)).

2. Retention & Rights

Attendee contact records are retained for the show cycle and required statutory accounting records. You have the right to request access, rectification, or erasure of your data, or withdraw marketing consent at any time.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.
