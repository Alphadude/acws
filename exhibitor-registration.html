ACWS Privacy Notice

Controller Identity: African & Caribbean Wedding Show (ACWS UK). Contact: privacy@africancaribbeanweddings.co.uk.

1. Purposes & Lawful Bases

  • Application Assessment & Contract: Processing necessary to review qualifications, allocate stands, and perform contracts (UK GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Event Safety & Administration: Compliance with venue safety, invoicing, and statutory accounting rules (UK GDPR Art. 6(1)(c)).
  • Curation & Aggregated Market Insight: Legitimate interest in maintaining luxury standards and compiling strictly anonymised market reports (UK GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Electronic Marketing: Optional promotional updates based strictly on affirmative consent (UK GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)).

2. Recipients & Data Security

Vendor data is held strictly on secure systems with restricted staff access. Identifiable data is never sold or shared with third parties for their independent marketing without explicit, separate consent.

3. Retention & Rights

Information is retained for the event cycle and mandatory statutory record-keeping periods. Under UK GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, or request erasure of your data.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.
